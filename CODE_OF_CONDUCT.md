# Community code of conduct

This project expects professional, respectful, and technically honest participation.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- state uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and material provenance;
- respect privacy, embargoes, security reports, and disclosure boundaries;
- give constructive, actionable review feedback;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized conduct, and targeted hostility;
- avoid knowingly misleading compatibility, security, authorship, or certification claims;
- respect maintainer decisions while using the documented appeal path.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, publication of private reports or personal data, deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith argument, plagiarism, license evasion, and attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe disclosure.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security-reporting path when public discussion would expose a person or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, request changes, restrict participation, lock conversations, or ban accounts based on severity and pattern.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid unnecessary disclosure. A participant may request reconsideration by a maintainer not primarily involved in the original decision when one is available.
