Micrantha Solution · Incubating

Overview

Dubnium asks how a developer workstation should behave when it is also an AI runtime, automation host, build environment, and governed operations surface.

This repository is the public community and distribution boundary. Trusted-operator configuration, machine-specific topology, secrets, and recovery procedures remain outside the public surface.

System themes

Rebuildability

Declared workstation, compute, automation, and development profiles built on NixOS.

AI control plane

Local-first model routing with intentional provider fallback and observable workload boundaries.

Bounded execution

Repository-scoped workflows, explicit capabilities, protected paths, and sanitized evidence.

Governance

Anthesis-backed policy decisions, approval gates, provenance, and reviewable outcomes.

Public integration testbed

The governed-agent demo exercises structured plans, deterministic allow/approval/deny decisions, exact approval binding, a capability-bounded executor, and fail-closed evaluator identity checks.

Project status

Dubnium is incubating. The workstation, AI runtime, automation, and governed-agent capabilities are being integrated behind explicit support tiers and trust boundaries.
