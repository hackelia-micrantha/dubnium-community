The Apache-2.0 source license does not grant trademark rights.

“Dubnium,” project logos, and future compatibility or certification marks identify the project and its official releases. They may be protected by common-law or registered rights now or in the future; this policy does not assert that any specific mark is registered.

## Permitted use

You may:

- refer truthfully to the Dubnium project or protocol;
- state that software implements or targets a named public Dubnium contract version;
- link to this repository;
- use the unmodified project name in factual commentary, education, comparison, or compatibility discussion.

## Restricted use

You may not use project names, logos, or marks to:

- imply endorsement, sponsorship, maintainership, official status, or organizational affiliation;
- describe a modified distribution as an official Dubnium release;
- claim “certified,” “verified,” “approved,” or equivalent status without an active project-issued program and authorization;
- create confusingly similar product, organization, package, domain, or service branding;
- misrepresent compatibility, security properties, support level, or conformance results.

## Compatibility statements

Until an official compatibility program exists, implementations should use factual wording such as:

> Implements the experimental Dubnium Capability Gateway contract version X.Y.

A compatibility statement must identify the contract version, stability level, tested conformance suite version, and any extensions or deviations. Passing a public test suite does not imply security review, production suitability, endorsement, or certification.

## Forks and modifications

Forks should use a distinct name and clearly state that they are unofficial. They may describe their origin and the upstream contract versions they implement.

## Permission requests

Requests for uses outside this policy should be directed to repository maintainers through a public issue that contains no confidential material, or through a private channel when the proposed use is not yet public.
